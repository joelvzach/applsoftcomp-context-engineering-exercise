## Ithaca + 7 days

Ithaca fits the $2000 budget comfortably at exactly $2000 total. Nature-focused activities work perfectly for ages 10-80 with accessible trails alongside challenging hikes. The college town means free cultural events and museums which stretch the budget further.

## Niagara + 7 days

Niagara exceeds budget at $2400 requiring either $400 increase or cutting activities. The iconic waterfall experience justifies the overage for once-in-a-lifetime trip. Accessibility exists for all ages via elevators and paved paths making it senior-friendly.

## Teaneck + 7 days

Teaneck stays under budget at $1900 making it the most financially conservative choice. The NYC proximity creates false economy as daily commuting tires elderly group members. Best for groups prioritizing budget over destination excitement.

## Buffalo + 7 days

Buffalo matches Ithaca at exactly $2000 with more urban amenities for seniors. The city's walkable downtown and lakefront appeal across generations. Architecture tours provide unique cultural value unavailable elsewhere.

## Rochester + 7 days

Rochester hits $2000 target with strongest museum offerings for children and adults. Finger Lakes wine access adds adult appeal but requires childcare planning for minors. The balanced urban-natural mix serves diverse interests well.

## New York City + 7 days

NYC demands $4200 doubling the stated budget making it unrealistic without increase. The unmatched cultural offerings justify the overage if budget flexibility exists. Walking distances and transit costs challenge elderly mobility significantly.
